Most of what I learn while doing computer vision research never leaves my lab notebook. I'm starting this site to change that — writing up the methods, failures, and design decisions behind zero-shot classification work as I go, rather than only after a paper is accepted.

## Why now

I'm in my final year of a PhD focused on zero-shot classification: getting models to recognize categories they were never explicitly trained on. It's a good time to start writing publicly — the ideas are fresh, and I'm also thinking about what comes next professionally, which makes a public record of how I think about problems useful in its own right.

## What to expect

Essays here will lean technical: architecture breakdowns, failure analyses, and walkthroughs of specific techniques, usually with code. Project write-ups will follow a situation/task/action/result structure so it's easy to see what a project actually accomplished, not just what it used.

More soon.
